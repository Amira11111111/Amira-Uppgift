ABOUT

GetHerOutTheClub is a game you play with your voice. A microphone listens to the room: the louder the sound, the further right the character moves. The dance floor scrolls forward on its own, so you have to use sound to dodge exes, spilled drinks and bottles on the way out of the club. When you crash, it is game over. The game also controls real stage lights during the rave.
