ABOUT THE GAME

GetHerOutTheClub is an interactive rave-themed game controlled by sound. A microphone listens to the room, and the louder the sound, the further the character moves to the right. Players must navigate through a colourful nightclub, avoid obstacles such as exes, spilled drinks and bottles, and try to escape the club without crashing.

I am Amira Smith, one of the creators behind the game together with Anahi. As a creator, I like to create fun and engaging experiences. I like to try new things, and I try to contribute in developing experiences/designs that have an exciting concept that leaves you wanting for more. I like to keep it simple but intruiging.
